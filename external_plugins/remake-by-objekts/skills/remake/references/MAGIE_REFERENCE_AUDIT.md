## Design lessons we can responsibly use
1. Author remains decision-maker; assistant does not overwrite personal idea in the name of formula.
2. Form a cumulative living synopsis, character cards, scene register and notes; allow branches and version history.
3. Make a novice's progress legible with genuine **evidence of decisions**, not faux % readiness.
4. Store structural questions alongside authored answers; provide exported text/Markdown/JSON and rejected versions.
5. Separate cinematic storytelling layer from advertising effectiveness, because MAGIE is presented as a story workshop rather than an advertising planning system.
