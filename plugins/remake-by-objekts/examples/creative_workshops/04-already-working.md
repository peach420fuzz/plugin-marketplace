# Creative workshop D — coherent minimalist motion piece: **no fix**

**Fictional counterexample.** The owner has a 12-second visual loop: white cloth unfurls against a dark room, catches a brief overhead light, settles back in exactly the same composition. Sound is a single fabric rustle. The maker's *stated goal* is a hypnotic gallery loop, not an advertisement. This is an authored description; footage has not been inspected.

## Correct first response

If the loop returns to its starting image and its one movement remains readable, the described progression already matches your stated gallery purpose: **rise → brief suspended form → return**. I would not add a dramatic obstacle, a character, logo, voiceover or faster cut to make it 'more engaging'. The question that genuinely affects implementation is the **reset**, not the story.

**Tiny exercise:** play the last one second followed immediately by the first. Does the cloth position, light direction and audio tail close without a detectable jump? The answer cannot be asserted without the actual video. If yes, the loop may already be ready for its declared setting. If not, fix the boundary with the smallest edit or controlled recomposition. Two alternative endings are not necessary because the creator did not ask for a rewrite.

**Department map:** screenplay and advertising NOT_APPLICABLE; animation / timing / sound / finishing MAY BE RELEVANT. No claims that 'white cloth is generic'; no unsolicited quote or studio contact.

## Distinctive teaching principle

A competent advisor should sometimes identify **nothing to rewrite** and defend the creator's intent. Production scrutiny need not invent creative dissatisfaction. Real final acceptance still depends on actual loop playback, projection format and audio requirements.
