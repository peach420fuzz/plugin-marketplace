# Music videos — direct this song, not an interchangeable short film

## Establish the actual assignment

Identify the track, artist, intended recording/edit and the author's concept from supplied materials. Recover a clearly identifiable public source through authorized tools where available, or existing project/chat material the user authorized; do not repeatedly ask for information already reachable. Verify the identity instead of selecting the first similarly titled song. A draft treatment referring to an official video is not necessarily a commission to copy it.

Keep four things distinct: the artist's published description; lyrics actually obtained; audio actually heard; and footage actually viewed. Reading a video page is not watching or listening. A streaming duration does not establish the intended edit. Published credits are attributable facts, not evidence of creative superiority. Never invent vocals, instrumentation, tempo, timestamps, chorus positions or the performance of an unheard song.

Unavailable audio does not block a useful treatment critique. Assess the supplied construction, state that musical fit remains open, and propose a source-faithful provisional route. Ask one decisive question only when a required choice cannot be recovered. Do not announce a production-ready timed video without the actual relevant track.

## Judge the chosen relationship between music and image

Determine whether this piece is principally narrative, performance, choreography, observation, a conceptual/formal development or a deliberate mixture. Do not impose narrative causality on every kind of clip. A chorus can repeat an image while arrangement, delivery, bodies, duration or context changes its experience. A repeated stanza is not automatically a redundant scene.

Where audio is available, connect proposed image changes to actual phrasing, breath, silence, arrangement and shifts of address. Where only lyrics are available, describe a textual structure without pretending to hear it. Distinguish illustration, counterpoint, extension and intentional dissonance. A literal image can be precise; a surprising counter-image can be arbitrary. Neither deserves automatic praise.

Treat the declared theme as a constraint to understand, not a verdict to echo. A sincere song about family, faith or waiting does not make every letter, tear or sunrise effective. Conversely, do not rewrite faith into secular reconciliation, historical danger into a work trip, or several generations into one biography merely because those are easier to organize. Explain why a particular proposed action serves this particular work. If a radical alternative changes these elements, state the change and supply an actual compliant route when the available source supports one. If you cannot satisfy the core requirement yet, identify that precise unresolved dependency; do not manufacture a compliant-looking version or quietly choose a different film.

## Turn a treatment into filmable development

For the supplied treatment, identify its governing promise and the one to three problems that prevent that promise from becoming an experience. If it promises distinct generations, ask what changes in their behavior, relations or formal treatment beyond costume. If it promises waiting, specify what is unknown, who knows it and how the image develops that condition. Do not require the person to be rewarded, punished or rescued.

A setup may establish atmosphere rather than demand plot resolution. If the proposed sale, journey or search receives causal weight, either make its function matter or remove/reframe it; do not automatically force a house-sale subplot to dominate a song. Ordinary furniture does not require a symbolic payoff. Props should not all become carriers of one preassigned emotion.

For a proposed rewrite, write the actual sequences and transitions at the requested scope. State what is retained, what is new, how the mechanism changes and what remains weak. Avoid exchanging a stock historical departure for an equally stock moving-house reconciliation. An estranged relative, convenient message, repaired object or shared chore can be a new cliché or an unannounced new subject. Scrutinize that change before presenting it as an improvement.

Map narrative information precisely at the ending. A message confirming that somebody is alive does not prove their return; a silhouette need not identify its owner. Decide which uncertainty is resolved and which remains. Do not call two devices logically contradictory when they answer different questions. Also do not keep stacking resolution, ambiguity, miracle and slogan merely to repeat reassurance.

## Direct performance and make a usable proposal

Decide whether the singer performs in the film, sings to camera, appears as an actor, or is absent. Respect a deliberately non-performance video; do not add lip sync by reflex. Check how any performance passages relate to the film rather than inserting them as coverage. Actor behavior must have a playable task and changing relation, not instructions to look profound or produce tears on a prescribed beat.

Give a feasible outline of locations, roles, historical states, practical transitions and any actual effects. Offer durations only as provisional allocations until mapped to the chosen recording. A costume epoch, exterior train or crowd creates production requirements; unfamiliar historical details need verification rather than plausible invention presented as fact. A new palette or more expensive camera movement cannot certify the concept.

A rehearsal or paper animatic can check geography, timing and a specified reading. It does not measure the clip's artistic merit. Do not call an AI rewrite, a webpage inspection or a synthetic dialogue an installed-plugin trial, an artist approval or independent professional acceptance. Retain the unsuccessful alternative and its specific defect in development evidence, rather than hiding it under a later confident verdict.
