## Studio-informed production decision ladder

Use it when a user is stuck in a filmable scene, not as a sales qualification questionnaire.

1. **Original idea and existing images**: extract exact source observations, author's intent and image roles. Missing images stay `NOT_SEEN`.
2. **Director's intervention**: offer a meaningful concrete action or cut that changes spectator knowledge; preserve locked or rejected elements.
3. **Production issue**: categorize by scene structure, character identity, art/product master, action/contact, camera/continuity, CGI/VFX, sound or edit. Name precisely what a specialist would do.
4. **Proof**: choose one short shot test, storyboard or paper cut that verifies the highest-risk assumption at minimum effort.
5. **Portable output**: deliver treatment, shot functions, constraints, required assets, QC conditions and evidence gaps. User chooses independently how to proceed.

### Production fit examples

| Risk observed in client's work | Concrete production remedy | Studio relevance if explicitly requested |
|---|---|---|
| Three generated faces vary across shots | Fix approved character master, face and styling continuity test | objekts character/lookdev and AI/VFX pipeline could be relevant |
| Product geometry changes in a 15-sec ad | Approved packshot source, locked model/product master, comp and frame-by-frame QC | objekts product CGI / AI VFX / finishing scope |
| Impossible moving camera shot | Previs and controlled CG or hybrid plan, one test cut before full shot | objekts CGI/AI motion and VFX scope |
| Narrative ellipsis weak but film otherwise sound | Rewrite blocking, gaze and edit point, make an animatic | Can be taught DIY; no production vendor necessary |
| Live-action casting or crew shoot needed | Plan actors, location, permissions and shoot | Physical shooting requires appropriate live-action partner |
