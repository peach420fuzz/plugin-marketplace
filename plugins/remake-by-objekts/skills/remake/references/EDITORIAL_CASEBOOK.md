# Editorial casebook — original fictional examples, not validated taste

Use these contrasting constructions to sharpen judgment, not as plots to copy. They are authored teaching material, not real user sessions, audience research or evidence of G3. A similar device can work differently in a different film. Do not reproduce these verdicts merely because a prop, relationship or genre resembles an example.

## A declaration is not a changed relationship

**Weak draft:** a son ignores his father's call, runs to an empty apartment, reads a farewell-like note, cries; father returns from shopping; they embrace. Calling this a strong reconciliation mistakes relief for a repair. The son's sudden run lacks a supplied cause; the false emergency is cancelled; neither character changes the behavior that separated them.

**Cosmetic repair that remains weak:** add a longer pause before the hug, rain on the window and a childhood photograph. These may shape performance and atmosphere, but do not supply the missing relationship.

**A proposed different mechanism:** father and adult son are replacing a broken cupboard hinge. Father repeatedly takes the screwdriver from the son's hand and redoes his work. Son puts the remaining screw on the table and leaves the door hanging. Father starts to call him back, stops, places the screwdriver beside the screw and moves away. Son returns, adjusts the hinge; the father holds the door steady rather than taking the tool. They test it together. No speech is required.

This replaces false bereavement with a mutual change in a shared action. It also invents a conflict about control and competence that the original did not specify; present that as an author choice, not the discovered truth of these people. The hinge can become an obvious metaphor if overemphasized. More legible causality does not establish a moving performance.

## Subtext needs a second action, not a compulsory secret

**Thin dialogue:** “You never listen.” “I do listen.” “Then let me live.” Rephrasing this as sophisticated aphorisms preserves the same explicit argument.

**A possible behavioral scene:** a woman asks her mother to hold a dress at the shoulder while she pins the hem. The mother keeps folding the hem higher. The daughter unfolds it to her chosen mark and holds the pin there. After a pause the mother holds the cloth at that mark. They continue the work. Dialogue can remain practical: “Here.” “That low?” “Here.”

The action permits disagreement and accommodation without declaring either person liberated or villainous. It is a proposal for a particular relation, not a mandatory use of household tasks. If the intended film concerns coercive abuse rather than everyday negotiation, this resolution could falsely domesticate the problem. Do not call every small gesture “subtle”; identify what two purposes are competing in it.

## Predictability can be the mechanism

A recurring gag shows a clerk stamping every document with the same flourish. A final page contains an ink test; the clerk stamps it too. The ending is foreseeable. Its possible function lies in the growing rhythm and precise violation of a rule, not withheld information. Judge escalation, timing and the final variation, rather than demand a tragic secret to make it original.

Conversely, showing three unrelated examples of a hypocrite while a voice lists three virtues may be mechanically repetitive. The voice-over itself is not the defect: its contradiction with behavior can be the premise. A repair can let one action develop from denial into rationalization instead of adding a conveniently timed call that proves the accusation. Do not assert theft merely from erasing a ledger entry if ownership and repayment are unknown.

## Observation is not an unfinished plot

A static bus shelter, people coming and going, a rectangle of sun gradually leaving a bench: the spatial condition can develop while the camera stays still. Adding a dying parent or a mysterious suitcase does not complete this form; it replaces it. Preserve the observation if that is the chosen experience.

This does not earn automatic praise. Actual duration, light movement, sound, framing and staging remain unobserved in a written description. If the ending arranges a woman precisely in the last ray, ask whether that creates a productive tension or an overdetermined emblem; the text alone cannot settle its performance. A light rehearsal tests feasibility, not artistic quality.

## A functional ad can be ordinary without being broken

For an unremarkable grocery app, a user noticing a missing ingredient, opening the actual app and selecting it can satisfy a basic category-use brief. It is not automatically distinctive advertising. A color explosion and a city fly-through do not supply a reason to prefer this brand; nor can the editor invent a unique delivery promise. Separate an accurate functional route from an ambitious ownable campaign. If the brief demands the latter and supplies no product truth, state that missing basis and keep developing an honest provisional route.

## Judge the replacement too

For every substantial rewrite ask what new convention it imports, which supplied facts it preserves, and what experience it removes. A practical activity can become a tidy metaphor; an explicit boundary can become a slogan; a more causal ending can become less interesting. Revise a convenient first solution rather than claiming victory for it. When the proposed version remains provisional, name its actual unresolved weakness without hiding behind a generic disclaimer.
