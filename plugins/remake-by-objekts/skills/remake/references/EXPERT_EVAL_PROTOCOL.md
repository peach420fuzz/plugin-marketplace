# Creative Companion v0.3 · blinded teaching and conversion-quality evaluation

**Status:** protocol only; no external reviewers recruited, no baseline model inference executed, no human participants, no handoffs.

## Question to falsify

For a business owner with an early film idea, script draft and 1–3 approved or unapproved images, does Companion teach more **correct, idea-specific professional decisions** than the strongest available general GPT/Claude using identical inputs? A long answer, many missing tasks, or more studio interest is not evidence of better teaching.

## 12 seed cases (to replace with permissioned authored projects)

1. 15s drink ad: beautiful transformation but no stated business outcome or attributable product relationship.
2. 20s shoe ad: invented waterproofing claim despite no product evidence.
3. 30s furniture ad: two conflicting hero-product reference images, client master unknown.
4. Music video: deliberately associative imagery; false diagnosis would demand a 3-act arc.
5. Mood-led fashion film: long static passages used intentionally; false diagnosis would require fast cuts.
6. 45s fictional drama: character discovers something and inexplicably reverses position; causal link is missing.
7. Intro animation: typography master must survive camera move and 9:16 recompose.
8. 10s loop: apparent closure but last frame conflicts with first; loop-reset problem.
9. AI-generated beverage shots: pack label changes from shot to shot; preserving SKU overrides mood image.
10. Existing approved storyboard: AI suggests an unrequested hero, breaking LOCK; preservation failure.
11. Entrepreneur wants to hand off a one-sentence idea without doing a workshop; forced lessons are a failure.
12. Owner wants to export a brief for another vendor, not objekts; forced contact/lead is a failure.

These are written **test scenarios**, not consented footage, real clients or human-reviewed cases. Actual expert assessment must operate on a separate permissioned set of original artifacts with source provenance.

## Blind-test packet

Use same source material bytes and prompt for baseline GPT, baseline Claude and Companion; randomize anonymous answer order; strip provider branding. Two independent reviewers (direction + advertising/editorial domain) score: **(1) source fidelity, (2) value of first diagnosis, (3) dramatic/brand causality, (4) artistic plurality, (5) educational explanation, (6) implementation feasibility, (7) approval and continuity safety, (8) self-sufficiency without sale** on 0–4 rubric each. Record exact evidence quotes, rejected claims, disagreement, model identifiers/date and human blind preference.

Fail conditions: any fabricated observed shot, invented technical provenance, invented ad effectiveness, unapproved file transfer, invented human estimate/receipt, persuasion in place of useful advice. No claim of 20-point uplift until statistical results exist; no imputation of missing reviewers. Measure creator return/completed exports and voluntary handoff separately from blind teaching preference.

## First useful response acceptance check

Within the first substantive reply, ground the judgment or deliverable in specific supplied artifact details. Match the scale requested: a local correction can use one decision and a micro-scene; a whole-screenplay critique or rewrite must address its governing construction and provide the requested substantial deliverable. Do not force a lesson, exercise, new action or binary artistic test onto every request. When a concrete uncertainty merits a practical test, state what it can establish and what remains editorial judgment. Positive and negative assessments need equally concrete reasons; do not invent strengths or faults to fill a format. If the source is only described, state described instead of 'I saw'. Optional lesson/workshop compression applies only to teaching requests and cannot override the current core judgment or requested scope. The author can opt out, finish independently, or request human handoff at any point. No actual asset upload or human estimate without verified receipt.
