## Books to vet/clear for later corpus
- Aristotle, *Poetics*: a tradition of action/causal plotting, not a mandatory film format.
- David Bordwell and Kristin Thompson, *Film Art*: tools for form, style, editing and narration; publisher edition/pages must be verified before any specific quotation.
- Walter Murch, *In the Blink of an Eye*: editing intention, not a rule that always applies; edition/page required.
- Judith Weston, *Directing Actors*: playable action and actor direction; avoid simulating casting expertise as factual field production.
- John Yorke, *Into the Woods*: structural analytical framework rather than compulsory schema.
These books are bibliographic **research leads** here, not text that has been ingested, licensed or fact-checked page-by-page.
