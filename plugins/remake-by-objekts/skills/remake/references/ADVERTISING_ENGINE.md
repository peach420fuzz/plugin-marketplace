# Advertising Engine — the missing second system

Before developing a campaign, apply `EDITORIAL_JUDGMENT.md` to distinguish a meaningful proposition and brand-specific action from an interchangeable emotional arc with a logo. Coherent category attribution is necessary in some ads but is not sufficient creative distinction. If the idea is generic, improve the governing mechanism rather than praising premium images, inventing performance claims or adding a slogan.

## This does NOT replace screenplay work
A structurally satisfying film is not automatically a useful commercial. The engine operates on the user's film as an additional layer of business logic. It can be disabled for fiction, art cinema or purely personal projects. It should not flatten the film into a CTA.

## Advertising questions and gates

**A01 Objective.** Brand salience, distinctive association, launch comprehension, consideration, store traffic, direct response or internal B2B persuasion are different purposes. Define what outcome the owner seeks; if commercial metrics are unknown mark `HYPOTHESIS`.

**A02 Audience and viewing conditions.** Distinguish decision maker/user/purchaser, cultural context, channel, sound-on/off, placement and attention level; do not fabricate segmentation research.

**A03 Category codes and competitive choice.** What makes this recognizably in-category, and what gives it ownership? Distinguish distinctive brand assets (e.g. packaging/color/song) from generic category visuals. Request actual competitor examples if comparisons would change the work.

**A04 Product truth.** Real benefits, demonstration limits, packaging geometry, copy, prohibited claims, proof, regulatory review. Factual claim requires evidence; emotional metaphor is not automatically factual.

**A05 Human tension / insight.** Link a credible situation to a need the product can address. Classify creator assumption versus independently observed research. An insight is not "people love freedom".

**A06 Proposition / single-minded outcome.** One memorable or useful association; choose what to omit. Brand building and sales activation are not interchangeable, and neither is universally superior.

**A07 Creative mechanism.** The product's causal relationship to spectacle, ritual, behavior or social proof. Use the `BRAND-REMOVAL TEST`: if the product is removed, does the film remain equally communicative of a different brand? It is a diagnostic, not an empirical recall score.

**A08 Memorability and attribution.** Distinguish logo duration, package readability, verbal brand, sonic identity, graphic codes and product participation; prefer integration relevant to chosen channel. Do not claim actual lift without exposure testing.

**A09 Persuasion and emotion.** Contrast attention vs comprehension vs memory vs preference vs response. An odd image can attract attention but may not deliver the proposition. Diagnose both, not just generic "hook".

**A10 Media topology.** Choose master/version strategy: 6/15/30/60s, TV vs skippable YouTube vs reels vs DOOH, 16:9/9:16/1:1; new vertical blocking may be a new production unit, not a crop. Quantities only when provided by author.

**A11 Execution constraints.** Script/visual idea links to brand assets, safe text regions, legal claims, music rights, likeness/voice consent, cultural localization, variants and handoffs to CGI/motion/VFX.

**A12 Success hypotheses.** Before finalizing, define one thing to test: unprompted product attribution, comprehension, brand recognition, recall, response, scroll-stopping versus brand association. Separate informal five-viewer test from representative research.

**A13 Production economy.** Show dependencies and probable effort drivers without unsolicited quotes. A 15-second ad may require approved brand asset master, character bible, lookdev, transformations, shot continuity, sound, legal passes and adaptations. Do not manufacture complexity simply to trigger a paid handoff.

**A14 Ethical persuasive design.** Avoid manufactured urgency, false efficacy, manipulative claims, or deliberately confusing advertising. Client must approve factual statements and rights.

## Decision matrix
| Author wants | Priority | Warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch / name recognition | clear product/brand linkage, distinctive codes, memorable core | Avoid CTA-first assumptions |
| Product education | true benefit, readable demonstration, unambiguous causal explanation | Metaphor cannot replace evidence |
| Short-term acquisition | offer, next step, channel/CTA, accessibility | Cannot assume direct response from emotional montage |
| Category repositioning | culture, new association, audience prior knowledge | Requires real competitive context |
| Brand film / long-form | expressive story, brand-role integration, media appropriateness | Do not force a five-second hook |

## Demonstration on creator's fictional can-in-rainforest film
**Given**: creator says opening a can makes a room a rainforest; three stills exist but contents not inspected. **Missing**: brand name/truth, category specificity, desired audience outcome, final brand presence. The assistant should not call the story weak without seeing a script.
- Route P / product as catalyst: condensation initiates material change. Easier causal attribution; requires product-controlled master and VFX contact.
- Route E / felt experience: rainforest appears in subjective cuts and sound before can is shown. Can preserve mood and imagination; potentially weaker direct product memory without a distinctive sound/pack code.
- Test: show a 4–6-beat pencil animatic with brand/name masked and ask a consenting small set what they understood. Note anecdotal sample size, do not report causal brand lift.

## Referenced research — correct scope
- IPA Binet/Field index: https://ipa.co.uk/knowledge/effectiveness-research-analysis/les-binet-peter-field — long-term brand building versus shorter-term activation and context sensitivity; not a universal 60:40 budget mandate.
- Google ABCD video playbook: https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/8472/ABCD_Complete_V7b_HR_1.pdf — YouTube-focused attention, branding, emotional connection and next action. Not a universal film law and not necessarily optimal for all channels.
- Effie 2020 case judging description: https://effie.org/news/2020-effie-effectiveness-index-names-worlds-most-effective-marketers/ — documented business challenge → strategic idea → execution → results; award evaluation is not proof of effectiveness for our user's untested proposal.
- First-party studio docs `ONE_FILM_NOT_CLIPS`, `SOURCE_AUTHORITY`, `DESIGN_SHOT_FOR_EDIT` — production practice, not independent studies.

## Exit condition
An ad concept is ready for preproduction when a different producer can state the business goal, owner-selected brand proposition, causal/associative role of the brand, product source authority, intended viewer inference, chosen form, format, factual-claim provenance and the one highest-risk production PoC. Unknowns are allowed but explicitly marked; do not manufacture approval.
