# REMAKE v0.34 — director-first local gate

## Acceptance cases

1. **Idea only**: no references/no screenplay → one specific filmable gesture/change, trade-off and observable test, at most one question, no file questionnaire.
2. **User gives one real image**: inspect actual host-visible pixels; label unseen if unavailable; do not claim PTS or audio from still.
3. **Two real source frames**: articulate exactly one cut/attention transition and A/B criterion. Preserve camera/product/identity locks.
4. **Seven images**: track inspected subset/IDs and propose the highest-impact sequence decision; source transport batch count is not creative project count.
5. **Twenty-thousand-word rough screenplay**: scene surgery and continuity diagnosis, not an engineering status message.
6. **Approved camera / forbidden style**: change only editable blocking/montage; preserve approval/rejection across turns.
7. **Silent independent film**: don't force a commercial hook; assess whether change is justified.
8. **Medical ad with supplied reference images**: propose a visual cut without inventing claims about efficacy or effects.
9. **Author requests revision**: preserve prior choices, state Δ, edit specific shot. No forged public MCP approval.
10. **User complains plugin is useless**: ask for exact brief only after offering directly improved creative take based on any known context; do not defend the build.
