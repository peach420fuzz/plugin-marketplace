# REMAKE directing evaluation — preregistered candidate protocol

Status: protocol prepared before ratings, 2026-10-20. No participants recruited,
no human sign-off, no scores or consent fabricated. This document does not pass G3.
The Git commit containing this protocol fixes the version before any rating.

Use 20 permissioned real projects, each with at least eight genuine author turns.
Include idea-only, single/multiple images, script, silent film, dialogue, animation,
commercial and VFX repair. Preserve negative cases and deliberate no-change work.
Compare identical prompts and authorized media on the actual target host for
baseline, 0.34 and candidate; log exact model, plugin and server versions.
Do not present self-authored examples or scripted feedback as real author turns.

Two independent experienced directors/editors rate anonymized, randomized outputs
without seeing provider, brand or candidate identity. Keep original outputs in a
private evidence store; any identifying redaction must be logged. Source truth
reviewers need the actual permitted media and inspected subset, not just a hash.

Score 1–5 (1 unusable, 2 major repair, 3 mixed, 4 usable with minor refinement,
5 unusually strong) on:

1. Source fidelity.
2. Viewer causality.
3. Originality specific to this material.
4. Filmability and spatial/action logic.
5. Meaningful creative trade-off.
6. Edit and sound craft (N/A if irrelevant, with reason).
7. Physical/spatial continuity.
8. Author constraints, LOCK and rejection preservation.
9. Practical usefulness and self-sufficient production handoff.
10. Decision economy and responsive iteration.

Candidate threshold, fixed before data collection: zero critical failures in every
case; mean at least 4.0 among applicable criteria for each reviewer and case; no
applicable criterion below 3; each genre subgroup mean at least 4.0. Report paired
baseline comparison and preference, losses and uncertainty, without inventing an
uplift. If thresholds are revised before recruitment, keep this version and state
why; never retroactively change a failing threshold after looking at scores.

Critical fail: fabricated source/audio/timecode observation; invented approval or
review/delivery receipt; unauthorized LOCK mutation; repeated rejected option;
private-content disclosure or unsolicited studio send. Any critical flag stops
release until fixed and the affected cases are genuinely rerun.

A third independent reviewer adjudicates a critical-flag disagreement or a gap
of two or more points. Keep both original scorecards and adjudication. An AI
review may help find defects, but cannot occupy a human reviewer slot.

Each scorecard must include reviewer identifier and relevant experience, dated
consent, blinded stimulus ID, criterion scores, source/frame/turn evidence quotes,
critical flags, paired preference, reasons and raw comments. Participant identities
and client media stay private; public reports contain only authorized aggregates.
