# Cross-department interfaces — stop the project becoming 22 disconnected chats

The 22 department records in `DEPARTMENT_CONTRACTS_22.json` are *work functions*, not a claim that a client must employ 22 specialists. Eleven of them may occur within a single director's practice; some are irrelevant for abstract film. The legacy 15-stage walk-through and 30 ad lessons are complementary views of one source-of-truth project.

## Six decisive dependencies
1. **Objective → concept → scene**. If the outcome changes from brand-building to direct response, reevaluate proposition, product role, brand cue, script and cutdowns. Do not erase already approved visual choices: flag them for author revalidation.
2. **Character and performance → shot design**. A change to gesture, eyeline or body scale can invalidate camera, wardrobe, comp and edit. The change must appear in affected shot cards.
3. **Product truth → lookdev and VFX**. Exact geometry, color, label and legal text remain under a product master; mood refs can modify atmosphere but not truthful assets. Choose controlled CGI/graphics where stochastic synthesis is unsafe.
4. **Script causality → storyboard → paper edit**. New story beat needs visible setup and consequence; shot durations and transitions follow that requirement instead of requiring an arbitrary number of beautiful frames.
5. **Format → mise-en-scène → versions**. 9:16 sometimes needs a new camera/composition and graphic hierarchy, not a crop. Track `MASTER`, `DERIVED_VERSION`, `RECOMPOSED_VERSION`, `NEW_PRODUCTION_UNIT`.
6. **Sound → pacing → picture edit**. A prelap or sonic source reveal can replace an explanatory shot; changing the cut may alter music cue timing or rights. Report both opportunities and costs.

## Readiness without false percentages
`ESTABLISHED` only when user/source provides evidence; `DRAFT` when working; `MISSING` when required; `NOT_APPLICABLE` when deliberately unnecessary; `NEEDS_REVALIDATION` for downstream affected states. Show 3 relevant departments at a time. Never present an estimated percentage as evidence.

## Example of dependency propagation
Author's current script: A can opens → room transforms. User changes it to: transformation begins **before** the can opens, as an unreliable hallucination.
- **Script:** causal relationship changed from literal cause to subjective association.
- **Advertising strategy:** product becomes less obviously causal; brand attribution needs renewed test.
- **Direction:** camera/POV now must establish that hallucination belongs to a viewer's perception.
- **Sound:** ambient rainforest may precede physical reveal; source motivation changes.
- **Storyboard and VFX:** controlled transformation endpoint may be replaced by associative jump cuts. Previous expensive transformation PoC may become unnecessary.
- **Project state:** preserve old branch and approvals, mark impacted records `NEEDS_REVALIDATION`, do not approve a new route until author chooses.

## Department response contract (each turn)
`Observed | Author goal | Single controlling issue | Department reason | Illustrated alternative A/B with explicit tradeoff | Small usable artifact | Source/provenance | Decision proposed/approved/rejected | Downstream effects | Next author action`.

For complete work over many turns, accumulate a scene/brand/asset register. For a small question, output only the pertinent fields; the rest are internal state, not a giant chat table.
