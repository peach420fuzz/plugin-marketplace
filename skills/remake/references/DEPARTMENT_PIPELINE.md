# Creative Companion · Applied department curriculum (private v0.2)

A project moves through **questions**, not 15 generic boxes. Do not imply one person must hire every department. The creator may enter any stage; do not reset work completed with GPT. State must track **evidence**, approval, and downstream dependencies. Each question must be taught through an example based on the user's actual idea.

## Running classroom example (FICTIONAL TEST SCENARIO, not a real client)

User: "A woman opens a new sparkling drink; her ordinary kitchen turns into an impossible rainforest. I have a slogan and three attractive images." The teaching problem: spectacle is not yet product-specific and the transformation has no clear causal action, transition rules or editorial endpoint. This is an example only—never insert it into unrelated user projects.

### 01 · Business brief / creative strategy
- **Question:** What must happen *after viewing*, and who must believe what?
- **Teach:** Distinguish awareness, differentiation, comprehension, purchase and response. An awareness film need not behave like an app install ad. A claimed product benefit requires verified evidence.
- **Apply:** If all the viewer learns is "jungle appears", they may remember the effect but not the drink. Make the sensory benefit the cause of transformation; test an alternative where the drink isn't named and see whether the idea still communicates the brand.
- **Output:** Objective, audience hypothesis, proposition, mandated claims and exclusions, intended distribution.
- **Exit:** Clear stated proposition and source of any factual claim, or flagged unknown.

### 02 · Consumer tension / insight
- **Question:** Why does this dramatic event matter to a particular person?
- **Teach:** Insight ≠ demographic adjective. It is a testable/arguable tension related to the product's actual role. Avoid projecting unverified customer research.
- **Apply:** The kitchen can signify repetition; opening the can interrupts routine. This is one possible reading, not an asserted universal consumer truth.
- **Output:** One tension and one competing interpretation.
- **Exit:** Owner selects a direction or decides the ad is product-led instead.

### 03 · Central idea / visual mechanism
- **Question:** What makes this film uniquely attributable and executable?
- **Teach:** Product truth × human tension × surprising mechanism is an ideation lens, not mathematical proof; test causal brand attribution and repeatable visual rules.
- **Apply:** Option A: carbonation escapes into room as physical condensation and foliage, then recedes toward the can (product-driven). Option B: the heroine imagines the rainforest before touching the can (subjective, less direct product causality). Explain tradeoffs.
- **Output:** One-sentence premise, chosen metaphor, rejected branches, testable visual rule.
- **Exit:** Viewer can explain what causes the spectacle and why that cause fits the brand.

### 04 · Story architecture / screenwriting
- **Question:** What changes because of a character's observable choice?
- **Teach:** Premise, want, obstacle, action, consequence, turning point, aftermath. Distinguish meaningful ambiguity from simply missing a cause. There is no universal demand for three acts in 15 seconds; even a one-shot film can have progression.
- **Apply:** "She opens can → jungle" is a demonstration. "She suppresses a tiny condensation spill → roots erupt through a tile → she deliberately releases the tab → space unfolds" is an alternative with action, choice and escalation, but costs screen time and complexity. Not a forced improvement.
- **Output:** Beat-by-beat visual action and viewer knowledge; dialogue only if necessary.
- **Exit:** Each action can be staged and explained via consequences, or acknowledged as intentionally elliptical.

### 05 · Advertising dramaturgy / brand causality
- **Question:** How does a viewer connect the story to the product without a post-hoc logo trick?
- **Teach:** Brand presence and attention hierarchy, memory cues, natural integration, format-specific CTA. Use Google ABCD as research-backed *YouTube-context* guidance, not a hard shot rule. Contrast brand-building vs immediate response.
- **Apply:** A can visible for 0.2s behind fern leaves may exist technically but not read. Integrate recognizable pack geometry into cause and conclusion, or choose an alternate brand cue when pack prominence would destroy drama.
- **Output:** Brand cue and product truth map, intended visual/social proof, prohibited claims, last readable frame.
- **Exit:** Product's role passes a brand-removal test, or creator knowingly chooses a non-selling brand film.

### 06 · Directing / actor action / blocking
- **Question:** What does the person physically do, see and decide in space?
- **Teach:** Action is not the same as emotion adjectives. Determine eyeline, body intention, objective, counteraction, reaction timing, where physical attention moves and whose viewpoint owns the scene.
- **Apply:** "surprised" is unfilmable alone; the heroine tries to catch a small droplet, sees a leaf growing from its landing point, then cautiously touches it.
- **Output:** Shot-level playable actions, character path, camera-to-subject geography.
- **Exit:** Artist can stage actions without guessing psychological narration.

### 07 · Treatment / editorial form
- **Question:** Does the idea need suspense, comedy, revelation, ritual, abstraction or montage?
- **Teach:** Genre determines expected inference and rhythm. Mood, quiet and duration can be intentional; do not accelerate for generic 'retention'. Compare actor-led, object-led and montage-led forms.
- **Apply:** 12s gag version: a quick kitchen-to-jungle rupture. 30s poetic version: atmospheric transformation whose sound carries the shift. The former sacrifices emotional development, the latter may not suit skippable inventory.
- **Output:** Treatment with intended viewing experience, durations as *hypotheses*, formal choices and exclusions.
- **Exit:** Chosen form matches audience/business purpose.

### 08 · Shot list / storyboard / previs
- **Question:** What new information or feeling does each shot contribute?
- **Teach:** Entrance state, visible action, exit state, attention path, match/action continuity, point of view, screen direction, overlay of audio. Drawings/images are not yet storyboard unless shot function and sequence relationship are specified.
- **Apply:** Existing kitchen image only establishes location. Need endpoint composition with can readable, transformation intermediate state, bridging action, and transition logic. Some shots can be eliminated if one controlled shot communicates them.
- **Output:** Shot cards with relative time, framing, visual function, camera/subject movement and edit transitions.
- **Exit:** Another editor can reconstruct intended sequence from shot cards.

### 09 · Production design / visual world
- **Question:** What is the authoritative geometry and material logic of the two states?
- **Teach:** Art direction controls spatial consistency, density, color separation, prop use, practical materials, transitions and what is omitted. Distinguish mood image from architectural/prop master.
- **Apply:** Trees cannot silently replace kitchen windows if preserving architecture is LOCKed. Define surfaces that transform and those that remain stable.
- **Output:** World bible, product master, before/after coordinates, material transition rules, reference authority map.
- **Exit:** Visual contradictions are resolved explicitly, not averaged from references.

### 10 · Character / wardrobe / performances
- **Question:** Who is on screen, what stays stable and what may change?
- **Teach:** Silhouette, facial identity, hair, clothing cut, fit, texture and continuity through light/weather/motion. A 'same girl' prompt is not an approved character master.
- **Apply:** If indoor wardrobe stays dry while humidity appears, decide whether this is deliberate surrealism or a physical-continuity error.
- **Output:** Character/costume master, continuity lock, permitted performance changes.
- **Exit:** Multi-shot continuity can be tested on representative shots.

### 11 · Cinematography / optical logic
- **Question:** What does the spectator know because of camera position and visual hierarchy?
- **Teach:** Frame size, camera height, POV, perspective, movement/parallax, focus behavior, light motivation, exposure continuity, key/fill relationships and plausible lens behavior. Never 'recover' precise focal length or CCT from a low-res still.
- **Apply:** A constant camera can prove one-room transformation, while reverse angles expand coverage but introduce architecture drift. Pick according to dramatic function and technical risk.
- **Output:** Per-shot camera/light intent and spatial anchor, not fabricated camera EXIF.
- **Exit:** Lens/camera/light choices have stated viewer or continuity purpose.

### 12 · AI / CGI / animation / VFX / compositing
- **Question:** Which elements must be exact, which may be stochastic, and how can they be integrated?
- **Teach:** Source authority, product/character truth layers, geometry, depth, contact, occlusion, shadow, reflections, motion, temporal invariants. Avoid brute-force single-prompt generation for locked product assets. For objekts routing, physical filming is external scope.
- **Apply:** Keep can mesh/label controlled, generate atmosphere and foliage selectively, composite contact/occlusion; test one 1–2s kitchen-to-growth transformation with exact can lock before multiplying shots.
- **Output:** Asset inventory, layer routing, minimum decisive PoC, acceptance/rejection criteria, budget risk drivers without unsolicited prices.
- **Exit:** Primary feasibility unknown is narrowed by a test or explicitly blocked.

### 13 · Montage / sound / music / pacing
- **Question:** Can the audience understand the image action and sonic cause/effect across cuts?
- **Teach:** Audio anticipation/lag, reaction space, diegetic vs non-diegetic sound, transitions, edit motivation and temporal geography. More shots is not automatically better.
- **Apply:** The tab's first crisp click can motivate the rainforest onset; bass/sub and insect ambience can bridge an unseen botanical transition, if the material supports it. A flashy transition without a readable can may obscure the brand.
- **Output:** Beat-aligned paper edit, sound event map, timing targets and editorial alternatives.
- **Exit:** A rough cut communicates causality without explanatory voiceover unless deliberately chosen.

### 14 · Graphic design / packshot / versioning
- **Question:** What must remain legible at each aspect ratio and on each media surface?
- **Teach:** Brand fonts and logo are controlled graphics; recomposition differs from crop; supers/legal copy, CTA, safe areas, product readability. Claims must be approved.
- **Apply:** 9:16 can require moving heroine and can into a new arrangement rather than chopping the widescreen image.
- **Output:** Graphics/copy lock; version matrix: MASTER / DERIVED / RECOMPOSED / NEW UNIT.
- **Exit:** Each version is plausible and has a clear production consequence.

### 15 · Rights / approvals / QC / handoff
- **Question:** Can this film legally and technically be delivered, and what has been approved?
- **Teach:** Client-provided material vs public references; rights in soundtrack, product, likeness and branded text; versioned approvals; uncertainty registers; practical delivery QC and human handoff. This is not legal advice.
- **Apply:** A commercial-looking audio track found on social media is not assumed licensed; missing label master makes pack fidelity unverified.
- **Output:** Source and rights list, approval/rejection log, unresolved decisions, delivery spec, confirmed handoff scope if user chooses.
- **Exit:** Unknowns remain visible; never misstate external rights or producer sign-off.

## Adaptive curriculum paths
- **Commercial:** 01→02→03→04→05, then whichever physical creative disciplines constrain execution, then 15.
- **Fiction short:** 04→06→07→08→09→11→12→13→15; skip 01/05 unless commercial goals stated.
- **Music video / fashion:** define sonic/visual structuring principle; script can be associative rather than plot; jump 07→08→09→10→11→12→13→15.
- **AI stills only:** clarify if user actually needs a moving film before recommending pipeline.
- **Direct studio request:** prepare partial state and preview immediately; no forced teaching, no fake obstacles, no required rounds.

## How to teach one decision (example)
1. **From your brief:** "rainforest replaces the kitchen after the tab opens" (USER_PROVIDED).
2. **What's unsettled:** whether the transformation is experienced by the heroine, objective magic, or visual metaphor (UNKNOWN); different forms require different blocking and transition control.
3. **Route A:** single locked camera, physical surfaces change; highest continuity clarity, harder geometry/control requirements.
4. **Route B:** fragmented subjective montage; allows expressive discontinuity and lighter compositing, but may weaken spatial proof that the kitchen transformed.
5. **One test:** build exactly one 2-shot animatic and let a viewer summarize what caused the change; record their interpretation; revise only if it fails the author's intention.
6. **Save:** user locks chosen route and source authority; rejected route stays rejected.
